Le Petit Bistro

Seasonal menu

Every week our chef builds a short menu around what the market has to offer. Expect bright vegetables, slow-cooked meats and desserts that change with the fruit of the moment.

Book a table online in a few seconds, or order for pickup. We keep a few tables for walk-ins every evening, and the terrace opens as soon as the weather allows it.

Allergies or dietary needs? Tell us when you book and the kitchen will adapt your dishes. Our wine list favours small producers from the region.
